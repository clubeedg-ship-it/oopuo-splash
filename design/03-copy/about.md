**Role:** Founder & Principal Consultant

## SECTION 3: How OOPUO Works

**Headline:**
Small by design.

**Body:**
OOPUO is deliberately lean. No overhead, no layers, no sales team. This means three things for clients:

**You talk to the person doing the work.**
Every call, every email, every technical decision goes through me. There's no translation layer between your problem and the solution.

**You pay for outcomes, not headcount.**
Fixed-price engagements scoped to specific deliverables. No timesheets, no "we'll need to extend the engagement" surprises.

**You get honest answers.**
If automation won't solve your problem, I'll tell you. If compliance isn't your biggest risk, I'll tell you that too. I'd rather lose a project than deliver something that doesn't work.
