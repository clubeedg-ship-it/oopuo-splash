## SECTION 2: Three Contact Paths

The contact page offers three parallel options, presented as distinct blocks or tabs.

### Path A: HubSpot Form (primary)
Embedded HubSpot free-tier form for lead capture.

**Form Fields:**

| Field | Type | Label | Placeholder | Required |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Name | text | Your name | — | Yes |
| Email | email | Email address | — | Yes |
| Company | text | Company name | — | No |
| Interest | select | I'm interested in… | Select one | No |
| Message | textarea | Tell us about your situation | A few sentences about your challenge — no formal brief needed. | No |

**Dropdown Options (Interest):**
- AI automation & websites
- AI-powered customer support

- EU AI Act compliance
