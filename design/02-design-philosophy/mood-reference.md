# OOPUO — Mood Reference & Page Signatures

## Visual Benchmarks (calibrate quality, don't copy)

| Reference | What to borrow | What to avoid from it |
|---|---|---|
| **Linear.app** | Dark UI precision, typography confidence, section pacing | Their specific icon style, feature-heavy density |
| **Vercel.com** | Technical credibility through restraint, code-as-design | Can feel too developer-exclusive for our SMB audience |
| **Raycast.com** | Dark theme warmth, clear value props, smooth scroll | Slightly too playful for OOPUO's regulatory credibility |
| **Arc browser** | Bold layout breaks, confident copy, asymmetric hero | Their maximal color energy — we're quieter |
| **Stripe Press** | Editorial quality, typographic care, content hierarchy | Not applicable to our structure but sets the craft bar |

## Anti-Patterns (never do these)

- Purple-to-blue gradients on dark backgrounds (generic AI aesthetic)
- Floating 3D orbs, abstract "neural network" graphics, particle effects
- Stock photos of diverse teams pointing at screens
- Inter, Roboto, or Arial as the primary font
- Centered everything — break symmetry intentionally
- "Powered by AI" badges or robot icons
- Walls of text without visual rhythm
- CTAs that say "Learn More" (say what they'll actually get)

## The "One Memorable Thing" Per Page

### Home
**The Split Path.** The hero presents two distinct doors — not generic buttons, but visually distinct cards or panels that make the visitor feel seen. One path for builders, one for the regulated.

### For Growing Businesses (SMB)
**The Time Saved Counter.** A single, prominent number (animated on scroll) showing hours saved per week through automation.

### For Regulated Companies (Enterprise)
**The Fine Scale.** A visual representation of what €35M means — not just a number, but a contextual comparison.

### About
**The Honest Photo.** Not a corporate headshot — a real, candid image of Otavio or a typographic portrait.

### Contact
**The Zero-Friction Form.** Memorable for how little it asks. Three fields, one dropdown, one button.

## Atmospheric Details

- **Noise grain**: 2–3% opacity, monochromatic, overlaid on dark surfaces
- **Grid lines**: optional subtle grid or dot pattern on hero sections (8–10% opacity)
- **Section transitions**: background color shift between sections to create rhythm
- **Selection color**: teal accent at 30% opacity
- **Scrollbar**: thin, styled to match theme
