# OOPUO Blog — Strategy

## Purpose
1. **SEO** — Capture search traffic from European businesses researching AI compliance and automation
2. **Credibility** — Demonstrate technical depth
3. **Lead nurturing** — Reason to return before ready to buy

## Categories
| Category | Slug | ICP |
|---|---|---|
| EU AI Act | eu-ai-act | Enterprise |
| AI Automation | automation | SMB |
| Technical Deep Dives | technical | Both |
| Industry Perspectives | perspectives | Both |

## Tone
Calm authority. Senior engineer explaining to a smart non-specialist. Specific numbers, real regulation articles, practical action.

## Format
800–1500 words. Clear H2/H3. Short paragraphs (3 sentences max). Lead with the problem. End with topic-specific CTA. One cover image per post (dark, geometric, teal accent).

## Cadence
Launch with 3 posts. Then 2/month (one compliance, one automation).

## First Three Posts
1. EU AI Act practical guide (eu-ai-act, keyword: "EU AI Act compliance guide")
2. 5 AI automations for SMBs (automation, keyword: "AI automation small business Europe")
3. Agent observability as compliance weapon (technical, keyword: "AI agent observability compliance")

## SEO
One primary keyword + 2–3 long-tail per post. Meta < 155 chars. Internal links to service pages. Schema: Article + author.
