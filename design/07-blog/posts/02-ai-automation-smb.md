You didn't start a business to copy data between spreadsheets. But here you are — Monday morning, manually updating the CRM because the lead form doesn't talk to your pipeline. Here are five automations we deploy regularly for European SMBs.

## 1. AI customer support that actually resolves tickets

A modern AI assistant trained on your product docs, FAQ, and past conversations handles repetitive questions 24/7. Not the clunky chatbot from 2019 — context-aware, multi-step, knows when to escalate.

**Time saved:** 15–20 hours/week for 200+ monthly interactions. Resolves 60–70% without human involvement.

## 2. Lead capture and qualification on autopilot

Form submission triggers AI scoring, CRM creation, personalized auto-response — within 60 seconds. No manual triage.

**Time saved:** 5–8 hours/week for 50+ monthly leads.

## 3. Invoice and payment processing

Automated invoice generation, scheduled sending, payment matching from bank feeds, reminder sequences at day 7, 14, 30.

**Time saved:** 4–6 hours/week for 100+ monthly invoices.

## 4. Report generation from scattered data

Scheduled workflow pulls from HubSpot, Toggl, Xero (or your stack), assembles a formatted report with AI commentary on trends. Drops it in Slack at 7am Monday.

**Time saved:** 3–5 hours/week.

## 5. Content and communication drafts

AI drafts LinkedIn posts, newsletters, proposal templates from your recent activity and templates. You review and approve — never start from zero.

**Time saved:** 3–5 hours/week.

## The compounding effect

Stack three automations: 25–30 hours reclaimed weekly. Almost a full-time employee's capacity returned to your team. Most deploy in 1–2 weeks. All pay for themselves in month one.
