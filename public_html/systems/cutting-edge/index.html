S.01 · Interior design production

An internal logic
flow, rebuilt.

Cutting Edge designs and produces interiors. The work was never the problem — the flow around it was. Sourcing, design, estimating and project handling each sat in a different place, and every job paid the cost of moving between them.

1–2 weeks per design → 2 days maximum

What changed: the AI does the sourcing and the design. Four workflows, one flow — each one below.

What it took

How it was built.

  1. Follow the existing flow. Not the flow as described — the one actually being run, including the parts nobody would put in a process document.
  2. Find where the time goes. The 3D modelling was the obvious cost. The re-typing between stages was the quiet one.
  3. Automate the production, keep the judgement. The AI sources and drafts. What goes to a client is still a decision a person makes.
  4. Hand it over. It runs inside the company, not inside a retainer.
