# OOPUO content pre-digest

**Status:** discovery material, not approved positioning or website copy  
**Purpose:** reconstruct what has already been written before asking the founder to start from a blank page  
**Sources:** current EN and PT-BR pages, Europe translations, project decisions, pricing, blog posts, portfolio additions, and the redacted automation case study

## How to use this document

This document separates four kinds of material:

- **Stated:** the existing content says this directly.
- **Implied:** the content points here, but the founder has not confirmed it.
- **Needs proof:** the site presents this as fact, but its evidence or permission should be verified.
- **Unknown:** the existing material does not answer it.

Nothing here should be treated as final merely because it already exists. The useful job is to identify the truth worth keeping and remove accidental strategy.

## 1. The company currently described by the content

### The shortest honest reconstruction

OOPUO is presented as a founder-led engineering studio that turns repetitive work, disconnected tools, weak digital experiences, and risky AI usage into working systems. It sells implementation rather than advice: websites, customer-service assistants, internal automation, integrations, and technical AI governance. Its stated operating promise is senior engineering, direct collaboration, fixed scope, visible progress, and practical outcomes without agency theatre.

That reconstruction is coherent at the level of *how OOPUO works*. It is not yet coherent at the level of *who OOPUO is for* or *what it should become known for*.

### The four businesses currently living inside OOPUO

1. **Operational automation studio**  
   Removes repetitive administrative work by connecting existing tools and building reliable AI-assisted workflows.

2. **Web and conversion studio**  
   Designs and builds fast custom websites and landing pages, with copy collaboration, analytics, multilingual routing, and monitoring.

3. **AI customer-service provider**  
   Builds multilingual assistants across web, WhatsApp, and email, with human escalation and ongoing review.

4. **Technical AI-governance consultancy**  
   Helps regulated European organizations inventory, assess, observe, document, and govern AI systems under the EU AI Act.

All four can be real capabilities. The unresolved question is whether they are four equal offers, supporting capabilities behind one larger promise, or different businesses for different markets.

## 2. What OOPUO appears to believe

These are the strongest recurring beliefs in the current writing.

### Work should disappear into systems

The recurring enemy is not “lack of AI.” It is operational drag:

- repeated customer questions;
- information copied between systems;
- invoices and reports handled manually;
- leads routed by hand;
- status updates assembled from scattered tools;
- knowledge trapped in documents or individual people;
- a business growing faster than its operating system.

The desired result is time returned to people and fewer things depending on memory, retyping, or heroics.

### Practical intervention beats transformation theatre

The best existing line of thought is: a company often does not need a “digital transformation”; it needs the few systems that remove the most friction. OOPUO repeatedly rejects long discovery phases, slide decks, vague retainers, and technology for its own sake.

### Implementation is the strategy

The content says the people who scope the work also build it. Working software should appear early. Systems should run in production, be monitored, and remain understandable after handoff.

### Automation should augment judgment, not pretend judgment is unnecessary

Human handoff, escalation rules, audit logs, monitoring, retraining, and recoverable failure appear throughout the content. The implied philosophy is not “replace every person.” It is “remove predictable work while protecting the moments that require human judgment.”

### Compliance should be engineered into the system

The enterprise material argues that governance cannot be reduced to paperwork. Documentation should match the code; decisions should be traceable; risk controls should exist in the system itself.

### Existing tools are usually the starting point

Especially in the Brazil material, OOPUO promises to work with the tools a business already uses rather than imposing a large replacement platform.

## 3. The impact currently implied

The website talks mostly about outputs and efficiency. Underneath it, a larger impact is visible but not yet articulated.

### Business-level impact

- Give small teams leverage without requiring a larger administrative headcount.
- Help companies grow without allowing operational complexity to grow at the same rate.
- Turn fragmented work into repeatable, measurable processes.
- Make sophisticated technology usable by companies without internal engineering teams.
- Let organizations adopt AI without losing accountability or control.

### Human-level impact

- Return time to people doing repetitive coordination and data work.
- Let staff spend more time on judgment, relationships, craft, and customer care.
- Reduce the stress created by work that depends on someone remembering every step.
- Give non-technical owners a technology partner who explains decisions in ordinary language.

### Societal-level impact — currently only implied

- Make AI useful to ordinary businesses rather than only large technology companies.
- Encourage responsible adoption: observable, governable, privacy-aware, and human-supervised.
- Create systems that increase human agency rather than obscure responsibility.

**Unknown:** which of these is the founder's actual desired impact, and how ambitious OOPUO intends to be about it.

## 4. Current ICP hypotheses

### Europe: three ICPs are currently mixed together

#### A. Owner-led or operator-led SMB

**Likely buyer:** founder, managing director, head of operations, or commercial lead.  
**Situation:** the company is growing, but manual coordination and disconnected tools are consuming time.  
**Trigger:** a process becomes painful enough to delay sales, service, reporting, invoicing, or delivery.  
**Desired outcome:** reclaim time, prevent errors, and grow without immediately adding headcount.  
**Relevant offers:** automation, integrations, AI support.

#### B. Marketing-led small or mid-sized company

**Likely buyer:** founder, head of marketing, or commercial director.  
**Situation:** the website no longer represents the company, converts poorly, is slow, or is difficult to change.  
**Trigger:** launch, rebrand, campaign, international expansion, or dissatisfaction with WordPress/agency overhead.  
**Desired outcome:** a credible, distinctive, fast site that helps generate or convert demand.  
**Relevant offer:** websites and landing pages.

#### C. Regulated or risk-conscious organization using AI

**Likely buyer:** CTO, CIO, CISO, compliance lead, legal lead, risk owner, or AI program owner.  
**Situation:** AI systems are already being used, but inventory, risk classification, documentation, observability, or governance is incomplete.  
**Trigger:** board scrutiny, procurement, a customer audit, a regulatory deadline, or deployment of a high-risk use case.  
**Desired outcome:** know the exposure, close technical gaps, and produce defensible evidence.  
**Relevant offers:** compliance assessment, risk mitigation, observability, documentation.

These ICPs have different trust requirements, budgets, sales cycles, and language. A founder buying a €750 website is not evaluating OOPUO in the same way as a compliance leader considering a ten-week AI-governance engagement.

### Brazil: a more coherent ICP is already visible

**Likely buyer:** owner or manager of a small or medium-sized business in Goiás.  
**Situation:** WhatsApp is central to sales or service; repeated questions, missed after-hours demand, and manual re-entry are creating bottlenecks.  
**Trigger:** lost sales, overloaded staff, slow response, or the realization that hiring another person will not fix the underlying process.  
**Desired outcome:** sell more, respond faster, and reduce repetitive work without needing an internal technology team.  
**Trust requirement:** plain Portuguese, close support, visible availability, an understandable price, and confidence that OOPUO will not disappear after installation.  
**Positioning already written:** “parceria, não produto de prateleira.”

**Unknown:** whether geography should remain Goiás-specific, which industries have the strongest founder access, and which first offer is easiest to sell repeatedly.

## 5. Current offer inventory

### Europe — websites

**Promise:** custom, fast, conversion-focused websites without WordPress bloat.  
**Included:** brand-specific design system, copy collaboration, analytics, monitoring, and optional CMS/i18n/testing.  
**Process:** discovery, design, build, launch and monitoring.  
**Current pricing:**

- “Website” — €750, one-week delivery, €30/month hosting and care.
- “Custom” — up to €3,500 for CMS, multilingual routing, more pages, and testing infrastructure.

**Existing proof:** a new uncommitted gallery names Solyx Energy, CuttingEdge, newhempshi.re, FixMe, 4seed, Sentinagro, InterActiveMove, Aquamarine, and additional recent builds.

**Strategic concern:** the very low entry price may conflict with the premium engineering, custom-design, and enterprise credibility asserted elsewhere.

### Europe — AI customer support

**Promise:** multilingual, always-available answers trained on the client's information, with human escalation.  
**Included:** content ingestion, tuning, channels, handoff rules, and monthly review.  
**Current pricing:**

- Starter — from €1,950 setup plus €290/month.
- Premium — from €4,490 setup plus €590/month.

**Claims currently presented as proof:** 68% of tickets resolved without assistance; first response reduced from four hours to instant; flat support headcount through 2× growth; 1.4× late-night conversion; 40% reduction in phone volume.

**Needs proof:** identify which results belong to real OOPUO work, whether clients permit publication, and whether context can be disclosed. Otherwise these should not appear as shipped results.

### Europe — internal automation

**Promise:** reliable background workflows for data entry, reporting, invoices, lead routing, and other repeatable processes.  
**Included:** process mapping, agent design, integration, monitoring, and audit logging.  
**Current pricing:**

- Single automation — from €3,500; one process; two-week delivery.
- Automation suite — from €9,000; three to five processes; four to six weeks.

**Strongest existing proof:** a redacted renovation workflow that turns an unstructured brainstorm into a priced, six-zone estimate, a colour-coded spreadsheet, a matching floor plan, and a client-facing offer website. It resolves 14 line items and a €66,900.90 total. This is the most concrete demonstration of OOPUO's thinking: take disorder, structure it, calculate it, visualize it, and deliver something a client can use.

### Europe — integrations

**Promise:** connect existing platforms so information moves without retyping.  
**Included:** data mapping, middleware, verification, monitoring, conflict rules, and readable documentation.  
**Current pricing:**

- Integration — €750 for two platforms, one-week delivery, no monthly fee.
- Custom — up to €3,500 for three or more platforms and central sync logic.

**Claims currently presented as proof:** month-end reconciliation eliminated; signed deals automatically creating projects and invoices; zero oversells; one patient record across three systems.

**Needs proof:** the same provenance and permission check as the AI-support metrics.

### Europe — AI governance and compliance

**Promise:** make AI systems defensible under the EU AI Act through engineering, not checklist theatre.  
**Offers:**

- compliance audit and gap analysis;
- risk assessment and mitigation;
- agent observability and tracing;
- technical documentation and reporting.

**Typical engagement described:** discovery and inventory, deep assessment, implementation and documentation, then review and handoff over roughly ten weeks; focused single-system work may take two to three weeks.

**Strategic concern:** this is a high-trust specialist offer. It requires demonstrable legal/technical competence, careful claims, clear boundaries with legal advice, and a different proof system from SMB implementation services.

### Brazil — practical technology partnership

**Promise:** handle technology so the owner can grow the business.  
**Core solutions:**

- WhatsApp assistant for repeated customer questions and after-hours demand;
- automation for orders, reports, and contact administration;
- integrations that keep sales, finance, and other systems synchronized.

**Current pricing:** projects from R$3,000 plus R$350–R$750/month for ongoing support.  
**Relationship promise:** close follow-up, improvement over time, solutions built around existing tools, clear scope, and plain language.  
**Current acquisition motion:** outbound/prospecting leading to a WhatsApp conversation.

## 6. Current sales strategy implied by the content

### Europe

The site currently implies an inbound, credibility-led motion:

1. A visitor arrives through the homepage, a service page, or educational content.
2. The site demonstrates technical capability and practical expertise.
3. The visitor selects a service or compliance path.
4. The visitor books a free call, requests an assessment, or sends an email/form.
5. OOPUO scopes a fixed engagement.

Supporting mechanisms already planned or partly built:

- multilingual organic discovery;
- blog content around automation and the EU AI Act;
- portfolio and case-study proof;
- HubSpot forms;
- a future meeting scheduler;
- Meta Pixel after consent;
- explicit prices on implementation services.

**Current weakness:** nearly every path collapses into “book a free call,” without giving different buyers an appropriate next step. There is no clear qualification mechanism, diagnostic offer, downloadable artifact, founder conversation, or distinction between a €750 purchase and an enterprise assessment.

### Brazil

The strategy is more direct:

1. OOPUO identifies owner-led businesses with visible operational pain.
2. Outreach or referral starts a WhatsApp conversation.
3. The owner describes the daily bottleneck in ordinary language.
4. OOPUO identifies a small first intervention.
5. A project begins, followed by a monthly partnership.

The website's role is therefore not primarily SEO. It is to reassure a prospect after outreach: this is real, understandable, locally relevant, and safe to engage.

### Strong traits

- technically capable;
- direct;
- anti-theatre;
- concrete;
- fast-moving;
- operationally minded;
- protective of human escalation and accountability;
- comfortable making complex systems understandable.

**Enterprise voice:** urgent, regulatory, formal, risk-driven.

The ASCII sculpture, HUD language, dark-to-light room transitions, cyan/warm palettes, and engineering-instrument atmosphere suggest a company that makes invisible systems visible. That is a potentially meaningful brand idea, not merely decoration.

### The largest brand omission

There is almost no founder. The visitor cannot answer:

- Who is behind OOPUO?
- What did this person do before?
- Why should this person be trusted with operational or regulated systems?
- Why Amsterdam and Goiás?
- Is OOPUO one founder, a core team, or a network?
- What personal conviction connects the two markets?

The site repeatedly says “we,” “senior engineers,” and “engineering team,” but never introduces the humans represented by those claims.

## 9. The central strategic tensions

### Breadth versus memorability

“Websites, assistants, automation, integrations, and AI compliance” proves range but gives the market no single idea to remember.

### SMB accessibility versus enterprise authority

Low entry prices, rapid delivery, and owner-friendly language attract smaller buyers. Enterprise governance requires specialist authority, long sales cycles, and institutional proof. Both may fit the founder, but they should not accidentally share the same funnel.

### Productized certainty versus honest engineering

Fixed scope and clear pricing reduce buyer anxiety. “No it depends” becomes risky when used as an absolute promise across genuinely complex systems.

### Automation efficiency versus human purpose

The writing frequently says “without hiring more people” or technology “working in place of” people. Elsewhere it values human judgment and escalation. The founder must decide whether the brand leads with labor replacement, human leverage, better work, or business resilience.

### Two markets versus one identity

Europe and Brazil have distinct offers and sales motions, which is sensible. What is missing is the shared founder-level belief that makes both recognizably OOPUO.

## 10. Preliminary founder truth draft

This is intentionally written as an editable hypothesis. Copy it, rewrite it, or reject it.

### What OOPUO is

> OOPUO is a founder-led systems studio. We take work that is repetitive, fragmented, difficult to see, or risky to scale and turn it into a system people can rely on. Sometimes that means automation. Sometimes it means connecting tools, rebuilding a digital experience, or making an AI system observable and governable. The technology changes; the job is consistent: understand how the work really happens, remove unnecessary friction, and leave behind something that works.

### Why it may exist

> Most businesses do not need more technology for its own sake. They need someone who can see the whole process, find the part that creates the most drag, and take responsibility for improving it. OOPUO exists to make that level of engineering available without the overhead, distance, and theatre of a traditional agency or consultancy.

### The impact it may seek

> We want technology to give people more agency over their work: fewer hours spent copying, chasing, remembering, and correcting; more time for judgment, relationships, and growth. As AI becomes part of ordinary business infrastructure, we also want that infrastructure to remain understandable, accountable, and under human control.

### Who it may serve

> We work best with organizations that have outgrown an improvised way of working but do not want a bloated transformation program. They can point to a real bottleneck, give us access to the people living with it, and are willing to change the process—not only add a tool on top.

### How it may work

> We start with the work, not the software. We map the current reality, define a small valuable outcome, build visibly, and measure whether the system changed anything. The person responsible for understanding the problem stays responsible through delivery. We use plain language, make trade-offs explicit, and do not hide behind decks or jargon.

### What it may refuse

> We do not automate a broken process without questioning it. We do not use AI where a simpler rule will do. We do not remove human judgment from decisions that require it. We do not sell a platform when the client needs a solution. We do not call a prototype finished because it looks impressive in a demo.

### Founder introduction — unknown, structure only

What bothered me was **[personal conviction]**. I started OOPUO because **[reason this company had to exist]**. Today I personally **[your role in discovery, design, engineering, sales, or delivery]**, working with **[team/network truth]**. The long-term ambition is **[vision beyond selling projects]**.
